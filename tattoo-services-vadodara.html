Which Style Suits Your Idea?

Custom tattoo design

Custom work is where we spend most of our drawing time. You bring a word, a memory, a picture from Instagram, or nothing at all, and we sketch something that fits your body and your idea. The consultation is free, and we change the drawing until you're happy with it.

Realism and portrait tattoos

Realism means portraits of people you love, pets, animals and objects, drawn with proper shading and depth. It looks best when it has room, so we usually suggest going a bit larger than you first planned. Bring a clear, high-quality photo. A blurry reference gives a blurry tattoo.

Fine line tattoos

Fine line suits small symbols, flowers, delicate lettering and minimal art. Thin lines soften over the years, so we pick the placement and line weight with that in mind. Fingers and hands are the toughest spots for it.

Colour tattoos

Colour suits flowers, spiritual art, cartoon-style designs and anything that should feel alive. Once it has healed, colour needs sunscreen to stay bright, so it's worth getting into that habit early.

Cover-up tattoos

A cover-up usually means a new design that is larger and darker than the old one. Some old tattoos can be reworked, and some can't be hidden completely. Send a clear photo taken in daylight and we'll tell you honestly what is possible.

Matching tattoos

Matching tattoos for couples, siblings or friends can be identical, or two halves of one design. Come in together, or send us the idea first and we'll draw it up.
