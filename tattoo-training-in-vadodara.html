What You Learn at Our Tattoo Academy

You can pick up pieces of tattooing from videos, but not the feel of it. At our academy in Vasna-Bhayli, Vadodara, you learn inside a working studio with one person guiding you, and you practise on live skin once you are ready.

The craft

Machine mechanics and setup, linework, shading and colour blending, and how to turn an idea into a design that sits well on skin.

Hygiene and safe setup

How a professional station is prepared, how needles and equipment are handled, and what a clean session looks like from start to finish. We teach this before anything else.

Studio workflow

Consultations, quoting, aftercare advice and running a session, so you understand the work around the tattoo as well.

Questions to Ask Any Tattoo Academy

Whichever tattoo training course in Vadodara you are considering, ours included, ask these before you pay anything.

  • Who teaches me? Ask for the name of the artist, and whether you will learn from them directly.
  • Is there live practice? Videos and practice skin are a start. Find out when and how you get to work on real clients.
  • Is hygiene taught first? Safe setup is the most important thing a new tattooer learns. It should not be an afterthought.
