Matching Tattoos at The Kala Culture Tattoos

Matching tattoos can be identical, or two designs that complete each other, such as two halves of one symbol, a lock and key, or a shared date or coordinates. They suit couples, siblings, parents and children, and close friends.

We draw the design with both of you in mind, so the two tattoos work together even when they sit on different body parts and are different sizes.

  • Good for Couples, siblings, best friends, and parent and child tattoos, whether identical or complementary.
  • Think about Choose a design that still means something to you on its own. Pick placements that suit both of you, and sizes that work for both body parts.
  • Booking You can come together, or one of you can send the idea first. Both people need to be there for their own tattoo.

Finished matching tattoo work is in the tattoo gallery and on Instagram.

How We Work on It

1. Share the idea

Send us the concept on WhatsApp: who it is for, any references, and where each of you would like it.

2. Drawing

We draw the pair so they fit together. You both approve before we begin.

3. Booking the slot

Two tattoos mean more chair time, so we plan a slot long enough for both. Message us first if you want to be seated together.

4. Aftercare

Each of you gets the same aftercare instructions. Matching tattoos heal separately, so follow them individually.

Price depends on size, detail and placement. See the tattoo price guide for rough ranges, then send a photo on WhatsApp for a clear quote. For healing and aftercare, read our aftercare guide for Vadodara's heat and monsoon.

Matching Tattoo Questions

Can both of us get tattooed in the same appointment?

Yes. Two tattoos take roughly twice the time, so message us first and we will plan a slot long enough for both of you.

Do we both need to be there for the design?

Not necessarily. One of you can send the idea and references, and we share the drawing with both of you before the session.

Do matching tattoos need to be identical?

No. Many couples choose two halves of one design, or two different designs on the same theme. We draw whichever you prefer.

How much do matching tattoos cost?

Each tattoo is priced on its own size and detail. Small matching designs usually fall in our ₹1,000 to ₹2,500 range, and you can see all ranges in the price guide.

Do you do tattoos for friends and siblings too?

Yes. Matching tattoos for friends, siblings, parents and children are all welcome.
