Tattoo Price in Vadodara: A Rough Guide

Every tattoo is priced on its size, style and detail, so there is no fixed rate card. Use this table only as a first idea. We confirm the exact number once we have seen your design.

SizeTypical designRough range
Small (up to 2 inch)Symbols, initials, minimal line art₹1,000 – ₹2,500

A realistic portrait, colour work, or a tattoo on the ribs, hands or feet takes longer and costs more. Send a photo of your idea, the body part and the size on WhatsApp (+91 9157714414) and we'll give you a real number before you commit to anything.

What Changes the Price of a Tattoo

  • Size Bigger means more time in the chair. Measure the spot, or cut a paper shape and photograph it on your skin.
  • Detail A small portrait with soft shading takes far longer than a bold outline of the same size. Colour packing adds time too.
  • Placement Ribs, feet, hands and the back of the knee are slower and more painful than an outer arm, so they cost more.

Which Tattoo Style Suits Your Idea?

Custom tattoo design in Vadodara

Most of our drawing time goes into custom work. Bring a word, a memory, a screenshot from Instagram, or nothing at all, and we'll sketch something that fits your body and your idea. The first chat is free, and we keep changing the drawing until you're happy with it. Read the full guide →

Realism and portrait tattoos

Realism means portraits of people you love, pets, animals and objects, drawn with proper shading and depth. It needs room, so we usually suggest going a little larger than you first planned. Bring a clear, high-quality photo. A blurry reference gives a blurry tattoo. Read the full guide →

Fine line tattoos

Fine line suits small symbols, flowers, delicate lettering and minimal art. Very thin lines soften over the years, so we pick the line weight and the spot with that in mind. Fingers and hands are the hardest places for it. Read the full guide →

Colour tattoos

Colour works well for flowers, spiritual art and cartoon-style pieces. Once it has healed it needs sunscreen to stay bright, so make that a habit early. Read the full guide →

Cover-up tattoos

A cover-up is usually a new design that's bigger and darker than the old one. Some old tattoos can be reworked, and some can't be hidden fully. Send a clear daylight photo and we'll tell you honestly what is possible. Read the full guide →

Matching and couple tattoos

Matching tattoos for couples, siblings or friends can be identical, or two halves of one design. Come in together, or send us the idea first and we'll draw it up. Read the full guide →

Tattoo Price FAQs

How much does a small tattoo cost in Vadodara?

Small tattoos up to about 2 inches, like a symbol, initials or minimal line art, usually fall between ₹1,000 and ₹2,500. Colour, shading and tricky spots push the price up. Send us a photo and the body part on WhatsApp and you get the real number.

Do you charge for the tattoo design?

No. The design chat is free, and we keep changing the drawing until you are happy with it. You agree the price before the tattoo starts.

Can I get a tattoo quote on WhatsApp without visiting?

Yes. Send a reference picture, the body part and the size you have in mind to +91 9157714414. For a cover-up, add a clear photo of the old tattoo taken in daylight.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Cash, UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), debit and credit cards, and net banking.
