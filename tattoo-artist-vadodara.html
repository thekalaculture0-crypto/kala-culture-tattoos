How to Choose the Best Tattoo Artist in Vadodara

A tattoo stays for life, so it is worth ten minutes of checking. This is what we would look at, and how we handle each point.

Healed work, not only fresh photos

Fresh tattoos always look sharp. Ask to see healed photos so you know how the lines and colour settle.

Hygiene you can see

New single-use needles for every client, clean equipment and a protected workstation. You should be able to watch it being set up.

A style match

Fine line, realism, colour and cover-ups need different skills. Pick an artist whose portfolio shows the style you want.

Design before ink

A good artist drafts the design with you first and changes it until you are happy. Our design consultation is free.

A clear quote

Ask for the price upfront, based on size, detail and placement. We give you a number before anything starts.

Real reviews

Read recent Google reviews and look at how the studio answers people. See ours on Google.
